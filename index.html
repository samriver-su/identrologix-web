Featured Solutions

Two problems we solve exceptionally well

Intelligent Account Correlation

Correlate accounts, even with no reliable correlation key

Traditional correlation needs a clean shared attribute, such as an employee ID or email. When that attribute doesn't exist, our AI matching engine weighs many weak signals together to link accounts to the right identity, with a confidence score and a clear explanation.

  • Fuzzy name and alias matching: nicknames, initials, transliterations, maiden names, and naming conventions such as jsmith2
  • Contextual signals: department, location, manager, cost center, account creation dates, and login activity
  • Confidence tiers: auto-correlate high-confidence matches and send uncertain ones to a reviewer queue
  • Learns from decisions: reviewer approvals and rejections improve future matching
Connects to SailPoint: approved matches are written back through SailPoint APIs or correlation rules, so the result is native correlation with a complete audit trail.

AI-Driven Role Mining

Business-ready roles in weeks, not quarters

Our role mining goes beyond raw entitlement clustering. It combines access data, HR context, and actual usage to propose roles that business owners understand and will approve, then keeps them accurate over time.

  • Hybrid top-down and bottom-up mining: clusters access patterns and aligns them with job codes, departments, and locations
  • Plain-language role definitions: generated names, descriptions, and the reasoning behind each role
  • Usage-aware: leaves out entitlements that are granted but never used, so roles start out least-privilege
  • Role drift monitoring: flags roles that no longer match reality and suggests changes
Connects to SailPoint: approved roles are published as SailPoint roles and access profiles, ready for birthright assignment, access requests, and certifications.

FAQ

Common questions

SailPoint already includes AI features. How is this different?

SailPoint's built-in AI capabilities are a strong foundation, and we help clients use them well. Our solutions extend them in three ways. They solve organization-specific problems such as attribute-free correlation. They bring in data that lives outside SailPoint, such as activity logs and tickets. They also support IdentityIQ environments and customers who haven't licensed SailPoint's AI add-ons.

Does this work with IdentityIQ as well as Identity Security Cloud?

Yes. We integrate through the supported APIs and extension points of each platform, so the same capabilities can serve on-premises, cloud, and hybrid deployments.

Will AI make changes in our environment automatically?

Only if you choose that. You set confidence thresholds for each use case. For example, you might auto-correlate matches above 98% and send everything else to a reviewer. Every action is logged.

How do you protect our identity data?

We design each deployment around your security, privacy, and data-residency requirements, including options that keep identity data inside your own cloud environment.

How quickly can we see results?

Most engagements start with a focused pilot on a single use case, such as correlating a problematic legacy application. That lets you measure results early, before committing to a broader rollout.
